Série: B

Section one: Reading comprehension                                               (5 marks)

Roberto Solano aged 24, from Mexico

Roberto came from Acapulco to New York ten years ago. At first he missed everything - the sunshine, the food, his girlfriend. But now he has a successful business with his three brothers and his sister. They run a soccer store in New Brunswick. Roberto's girlfriend is now his wife, and they have two children who go to American schools. When asked why he came to the US, Roberto says without hesitation, 'Because I want to work hard and be successful.' He certainly works hard. He's at the store all day, then works as a driver in the evening. That's why I like America,' he says. 'You can be what you want.' 'When I first came here, I didn't speak the language, and it was winter, it was so cold! There was snow! Now nearly all my family are here, not only in New York, but also in California, and in Texas. We meet about once a month and have a huge Mexican meal that takes about five hours! We're all happy here.'

After Reading the text, answer the questions that follow:

1 What did he first miss from Acapulco when he started living in the USA?

2 Why did Roberto move to the United State of America?

3 What’s Roberto with his Brothers and sisters’ business in New York- the USA?

4 What was Roberto’s first problem when he went to the USA?

5  Where are his Family currently?

Section two: Culture shock                                                      (5 marks)

Which stage of culture shock are these situations about Roberto? One stage is left out.

Honeymoon stage, crisis stage, adjustment stage, adaptation stage
