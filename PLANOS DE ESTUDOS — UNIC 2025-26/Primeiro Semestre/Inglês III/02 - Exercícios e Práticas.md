## SÉRIE A — *Culture Shock*

### Section one: Reading comprehension *(5 marks)*

> Texto de apoio: Kalervo Oberg divide o *culture shock* em 4 fases — *honeymoon stage, crisis stage, adjustment stage, adaptation stage* — mais o *reverse culture shock* ao regressar a casa.

**1. What are the phases of culture shock people face when moving to a new place?**
The Canadian anthropologist Kalervo Oberg suggested **four phases**: the **honeymoon stage**, the **crisis stage**, the **adjustment stage** and the **adaptation stage**.

**2. What stage do people face when they start confronting the new culture with their own culture?**
The **adjustment stage** — "People begin to accept the differences between the host culture and their own" and start to overcome the challenges of customs and language. *(É neste estágio que a comparação entre as duas culturas se torna consciente e produtiva; na crise essa comparação ainda é apenas fonte de frustração.)*

**3. What happens when people get back to their homeland after a long time away?**
They may experience **reverse culture shock**: they expect their country to feel unfamiliar, but are surprised to find that **many things have changed** and that **they themselves are not the same person**. Friends and family may not know how to respond to their new attitudes, values and habits, so it takes time to rebuild relationships.

**4. Which stage do you think to be the hardest? Why?** *(opinião — resposta-modelo)*
In my opinion, the **crisis stage** is the hardest, **because** the excitement of the honeymoon phase fades and negative feelings — frustration, anxiety and homesickness — reach their peak. The person still struggles with the language barrier and unfamiliar customs, **but** has not yet developed any strategy to cope with them.

## SÉRIE F — *Hetty Green, "The Witch of Wall Street"*

### Section one: Reading comprehension *(5 marks)*

**1. What kind of person was Hetty Green?**
She was a **very spoilt only child** who grew up to be an **extremely rich but mean (stingy) woman** — "one of the richest and most hated women in the world", nicknamed **"The Witch of Wall Street"**. Her meanness was legendary: she always argued about prices and saved every penny.

**2. Where did she find food to feed her dog?**
At the **local grocery store**, where she walked to get a **free bone** for her much-loved dog, Dewey (and to buy cheap broken cookies).

**3. How did she become a millionaire?**
From the age of two her father **taught her about stocks and shares**; when he died (she was 21) she **inherited $7.5 million**, went to New York and **invested on Wall Street**.

**4. What was the meanest thing Hetty did?**
When her son **Ned injured his knee, she refused to pay for a doctor** and spent hours looking for free medical help; **in the end his leg was amputated**.

**5. What happened to all Hetty's fortune after she died?**
She died in 1916 and **left her two children $100 million** (worth about $9.3 billion today). Her **daughter built a hospital** with her share of the money.
