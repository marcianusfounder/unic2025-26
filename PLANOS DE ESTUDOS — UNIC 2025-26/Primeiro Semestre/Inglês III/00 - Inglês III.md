## 🎴 Baralho de Flashcards Rápidos (Active Recall)

> [!tip] Flashcard 1: Definite, Indefinite and Zero Article
> **P:** When do we use the Definite Article ("The"), Indefinite ("A/An") and Zero Article (Ø)?
> **R:**
> - **A / An:** Singular countable nouns mentioned for the first time or non-specific (ex: *"A computer", "An algorithm"*).
> - **The:** Specific nouns known to both speaker and listener, or unique things (ex: *"The Internet", "The database we configured"*).
> - **Zero Article (Ø):** General plural nouns, uncountable concepts, names of countries/languages (ex: *"Ø Technology changes lives", "We study Ø Computer Science"*).

> [!tip] Flashcard 2: Passive Voice in Technical English
> **P:** How is the Passive Voice formed and why is it preferred in technical reports?
> **R:** Formed with **[Subject] + [Verb To Be] + [Past Participle]**. It is preferred because technical writing emphasizes the **action or result** rather than the person who performed it (ex: *"The data was encrypted using RSA"* instead of *"The developer encrypted the data"*).

> [!tip] Flashcard 3: Conditionals in Engineering Logic
> **P:** What is the structure of the First and Second Conditionals?
> **R:**
> - **First Conditional (Real/Probable):** `If + Present Simple, will + Verb` (ex: *"If the server crashes, the backup will activate"*).
> - **Second Conditional (Hypothetical):** `If + Past Simple, would + Verb` (ex: *"If we had unlimited bandwidth, we would stream uncompressed video"*).
