Section One: Comprehension
1- How do you define culture in your own words?
Culture is the set of values, beliefs, customs, knowledge, language, and practices that characterize a group of people and guide their way of life.
2- Explain the difference between tradition and culture.
Culture is the broader system of values, ideas, and practices shared by a society.
Tradition refers to specific customs or practices passed down from generation to generation within that culture.
3- Explain the difference between Emancipation and Democracy.
Emancipation means gaining freedom from restrictions, oppression, or dependence (for example, the abolition of slavery).
Democracy is a political system in which citizens have the right to participate in decision-making, usually through voting and representation.
4- How do animals communicate among themselves? Give some examples.
Animals communicate through sounds, body language, chemical signals, and visual displays. For example:
Bees dance to indicate the location of food.
Birds sing to attract mates or mark territory.
Dogs bark or wag their tails to express emotions.
Dolphins use whistles and clicks to coordinate in groups.
