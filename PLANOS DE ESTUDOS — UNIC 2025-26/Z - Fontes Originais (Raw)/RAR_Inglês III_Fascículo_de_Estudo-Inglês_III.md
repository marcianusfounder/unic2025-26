1) Tell about a tradition your family shares.
One tradition my family shares is gathering every Sunday for a special meal together. It helps us strengthen our bonds and maintain unity.
2) Have you ever had a friend whose family had different beliefs than your own? If so, what was your reaction to the difference?
Yes, I have had friends with different beliefs. My reaction was respect and curiosity — I tried to understand their perspective and appreciated the diversity.
3) What would your art tell someone about your culture?
My art would show themes of family, community, and faith. It would reflect the importance of unity, traditions, and values in my culture.
4) Do you speak more than one language? What is the value of learning an additional language?
Yes, I speak more than one language. Learning an additional language allows us to communicate with more people, understand different cultures, and build stronger connections across societies.
5) How can we help prevent wars between cultures?
We can help prevent wars by promoting dialogue, respect, and appreciation of differences. Education, cultural exchange, and peaceful communication are key to avoiding conflicts.
